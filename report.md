# Tell me about a problem

One sentence is enough. Say the chapter, your computer (Windows or Mac, and its version if you know it) and what you saw.

- Open a new issue on this repository (the Issues tab, then New issue), or
- email ramthamedia@gmail.com.

Never send passwords, account numbers or private documents. Every confirmed fix is listed on the [Updates](updates.md) page with its date and goes into the next update of the book.
